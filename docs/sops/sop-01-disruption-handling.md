# SOP-01 Disruption Handling

## Triage
When a flight is delayed more than 60 minutes or cancelled, the operations
desk opens a disruption case. Classify the cause first: weather (WX),
maintenance (MX), crew (CREW), or air-traffic control (ATC).

## Passenger communication
Notify affected passengers within 30 minutes of the disruption decision.
State the cause code, the expected new departure, and the rebooking options.

## Case ownership
One duty manager owns each disruption case until every misconnected
passenger is rebooked or refunded. The case stays open in the audit log.
