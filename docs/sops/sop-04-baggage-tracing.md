# SOP-04 Baggage Tracing

## Mishandled bags
A bag is mishandled when it does not travel on its passenger's flight.
Tag it immediately and open a tracing case with the last known location.

## Tracing
Check the origin sort facility first, then the transfer belt logs. Update
the case at every handoff. A bag is closed only when delivered or when
the passenger confirms receipt.

## Compensation
For delays over 12 hours, the passenger is eligible for an essentials
allowance per the customer commitment document.
