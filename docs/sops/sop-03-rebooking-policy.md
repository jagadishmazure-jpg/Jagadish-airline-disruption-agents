# SOP-03 Rebooking Policy

## Priority order
Rebook premium-cabin (J) passengers first, then passengers with the
longest misconnection delay. Never split a single booking across
different recovery flights without passenger consent.

## Recovery options
Offer the lowest-score recovery itinerary first. Explain the trade-off:
earlier arrival versus cabin downgrade versus connection risk. The
passenger's choice is recorded.

## Refunds
If no recovery itinerary arrives within 6 hours of the original plan,
the passenger may take a full refund instead of rebooking.
